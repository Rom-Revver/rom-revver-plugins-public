# Evo Flash Workflow

How ROM Revver fits an Evo tuning workflow alongside ECUFlash and a Tactrix cable.

## What this plugin does

Explains where ROM Revver sits in the Evo loop: it's the **editor**, while
reading and flashing stay with ECUFlash and a Tactrix OpenPort cable. Walks
through reading your ECU, keeping a backup, opening the `.bin` here against
your ECUFlash-format definitions, editing, and flashing the result back with
ECUFlash.

## Why install it

- A quick reference for anyone new to combining ROM Revver with an existing
  ECUFlash-based Evo workflow.

## Capabilities

None — display-only. It never changes your ROM.
